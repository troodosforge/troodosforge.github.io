Troopbound: Squad Shooter

Privacy policy

How Troopbound handles gameplay information, support messages, and privacy choices.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Troopbound: Squad Shooter (“Troopbound”, “the game”) is published under the name Troodos Forge, the publishing name of an individual game developer based in Cyprus. The developer operating under that name is responsible for the personal information handled directly for the game, including support and privacy correspondence.

For privacy inquiries or requests, contact troodosforge@gmail.com.

2. Current game data practices

Troopbound is currently an offline-focused game. The Android version reviewed for this policy does not require an account or login and does not include Google AdMob, an in-app advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, a developer-operated crash-reporting upload service, or in-app purchasing.

The game saves information on your device so it can remember your progress and preferences. Depending on the features you use, this includes campaign and mission progress, gameplay settings, audio and visual preferences, and tutorial progress. The game uses this information to function and to restore your experience when you return.

These game saves are stored locally. The reviewed version does not send them to a Troodos Forge server or use them to create an online player profile. Troodos Forge does not operate a gameplay-data collection server for this version.

The reviewed version does not request device location, camera, or microphone access for gameplay. Some information may be handled separately by Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, or services you choose to use; their practices are not controlled by Troodos Forge.

3. Advertising

Advertising is not active in the Android version reviewed for this policy. Troodos Forge intends to consider optional rewarded ads and possibly occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob in a later version. Describing this plan does not mean that the current version displays ads or transmits advertising data.

If an updated version includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google and participating advertising partners may process information such as IP addresses, advertising or app-related identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. The information processed will depend on the actual SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

Before releasing a version with advertising, this policy and the game's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be reviewed and updated to match the implementation. Any consent interface required for the regions in which the game is offered must also be implemented before advertising is served.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Emails and support requests

If you email Troodos Forge, the information you choose to provide may include your email address, name or display name, the contents of your message, and any files or technical details you attach. We use it to answer questions, provide support, investigate issues you report, respond to privacy requests, and maintain necessary correspondence.

Our grounds for handling this correspondence under applicable data-protection law are our legitimate interests in responding to users and maintaining the game, and, where relevant, compliance with legal obligations. We consider these interests alongside your privacy rights. You do not need to email us to play Troopbound.

Correspondence is processed through our email provider, Google, and may be processed in countries outside Cyprus or the European Economic Area under the provider's applicable data-protection arrangements. See the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Sharing and security

Troodos Forge does not sell player information or local game saves. In the currently reviewed version, we do not share locally stored gameplay progress with an advertising network or analytics provider.

Support correspondence may be handled by our email provider, and information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. We take reasonable steps to limit access to correspondence and protect the systems we control, although no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Keeping and deleting information

Locally saved progress and settings remain on your device until removed using available game or device controls. Clearing the game's app storage can remove local saves. Uninstalling the game may also remove them, although device backup and restoration features can affect whether some information is retained or restored. Troodos Forge cannot remotely access or erase local save files.

We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the matter, handle follow-up requests, maintain necessary records, or satisfy legal obligations. We periodically review whether continued retention is necessary and delete information when it is no longer needed for those purposes. If you want us to assess deletion of correspondence you sent, contact us at the email address below.

7. Your privacy rights and choices

Where data-protection law applies, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability where applicable. You can contact troodosforge@gmail.com to exercise these rights. We may need information to verify a request and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You can manage local game files through the controls available on your device. Because Troodos Forge does not receive those files in the reviewed version, we cannot retrieve or remove them remotely.

If a future version offers advertising consent choices, you will be able to make those choices through the consent mechanism provided in that version, where required. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

You may also lodge a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority. In Cyprus, this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

8. Age suitability

Troopbound is intended for players aged 16 and older and is not designed or marketed for children under 16. The age group stated here is the intended audience, not a formal Google Play or PEGI content rating. The reviewed version does not ask players to create personal profiles.

9. External services and websites

The game may be downloaded or updated through Google Play, which has its own information practices. The Troodos Forge website and this policy page are hosted using GitHub Pages. When visiting the website, your browser connects to GitHub's hosting infrastructure; GitHub handles information about those connections under its own privacy statement. These external services are not operated by Troodos Forge.

See Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Troopbound, including new services or advertising features, or changes in applicable requirements. The revised policy and its updated date will be published at this address. Where legally required, we will also provide additional notice or request consent.
