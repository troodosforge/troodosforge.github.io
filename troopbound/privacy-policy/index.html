Troopbound: Squad Shooter

Privacy policy

How Troopbound handles local gameplay data, advertising, consent and support correspondence.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Publisher and contact

Troopbound: Squad Shooter (“Troopbound” or “the game”) is published by an individual developer based in Cyprus under the publishing name Troodos Forge. The developer operating under that name is responsible for personal data handled directly for the game, such as support and privacy correspondence.

For privacy inquiries and requests, email troodosforge@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Troopbound does not require you to create an account or sign in. The game stores campaign progress, mission progress, preferences, tutorial progress and other gameplay state locally on your device. An optional reward in Endless Operations may be held in temporary game memory until the next operation; it may be lost if the app process ends.

Troodos Forge does not operate a server that receives these local gameplay saves. They are not uploaded to Troodos Forge as part of normal gameplay. Android or device backup features, if enabled by the device, may handle app data separately under the relevant provider’s practices.

3. Advertising and Google SDKs

The Android advertising-enabled test build includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Next-Gen SDK 1.4.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK 4.0.0. This test build is configured to request Google demonstration ads only; it does not request live production advertisements. An eligible player may choose to watch a rewarded test ad after completing an Endless Operation to receive one extra grenade for the next operation. Campaign missions do not currently offer this bonus. Interstitial ads are disabled, and there are no banner ads.

Although test ads are used, the Google advertising and consent SDKs are present in the app. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information, including an IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), device or other identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID where available, app interactions (such as launches, taps and ad views), and diagnostic information (such as performance and crash-related metrics). Google describes use of this information for advertising, analytics or measurement, and fraud prevention or security. Actual processing depends on the SDK, device and consent state. The Android build requests advertising-related permissions, including access to the advertising ID.

For more information about Google’s processing, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance.

Troodos Forge does not receive a copy of your locally saved campaign progress through the AdMob integration. Advertising-related data is processed by Google and, where applicable, advertising partners under their own privacy practices and the choices available to you.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Troopbound uses Google’s UMP consent system to request and record advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. The game checks whether the SDK permits ad requests; if it does not, the game does not request ads. If privacy options are required for your device or region, you can reopen them through Settings → Legal & Privacy.

Consent choices may affect whether ads are available or how advertising data is processed. You can play without watching rewarded ads. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You may also be able to reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device settings.

Publication of a regional consent message does not itself guarantee that a form appears on every device; whether one is required depends on the SDK’s determination, location and other circumstances.

5. Support emails and correspondence

If you email Troodos Forge, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, display name, message, attachments and technical details. We use it to respond to support requests, investigate issues and handle privacy inquiries. Emailing us is optional and is not required to play.

Depending on the request, our legal basis for processing correspondence is our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the game, or compliance with a legal obligation. Correspondence is handled through our email provider, Google, and may involve processing outside Cyprus or the European Economic Area subject to applicable safeguards.

6. Data sharing, security and international processing

Local gameplay saves are not transmitted to Troodos Forge. Google and potentially its advertising partners process advertising and consent-related data as described above. Support messages are processed by our email provider and may be disclosed when legally required.

Google states that data transferred by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Advertising and email services may process information internationally under their applicable policies and safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game progress and settings generally remain on your device until you remove them through available game or Android storage controls. Clearing app storage may permanently erase progress. Uninstalling the app may also remove local data, subject to device backup and restore behavior. Troodos Forge cannot remotely retrieve or delete locally stored saves.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to address requests, maintain necessary records or comply with legal obligations, and review it for deletion when no longer needed. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us.

Google controls retention and deletion of information processed through its advertising and consent services under its applicable policies. We cannot directly erase Google's records on your behalf, but we can help identify the relevant privacy controls and request channels.

8. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under EU and other data-protection laws, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of personal data we control, object to certain processing, and request data portability where the law provides it. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact troodosforge@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify your request and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You may lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. In Cyprus, the relevant authority is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

9. Intended audience

Troopbound is intended for players aged 16 and older. It is not designed for children under 13, and Troodos Forge does not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13. Age-related advertising and consent requirements may vary by country. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the game, its advertising features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this address with a revised date. The exact advertising features and data practices of a distributed version may differ from those of an earlier ad-free build; the description above addresses the advertising-enabled Android test build and its current configuration.
